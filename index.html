How it was built

  1. Clean the zerosGlucose, blood pressure, skin thickness, insulin and BMI contained zeros that are not physically possible. Each was replaced with that column's median.
  2. Scale the measurementsSeven numeric columns were squeezed into a 0–1 range with a min-max scaler. Pregnancies was left as a raw count.
  3. Split the data80% of the rows (614) for training and 20% (154) for testing, with random_state=45.
  4. Fit logistic regressionscikit-learn's LogisticRegression learned one weight per measurement. The estimate is the sigmoid of the weighted sum, a number between 0 and 1.
  5. Check it on unseen patientsAccuracy, precision, recall, F1 and AUC, as in the section above. This page runs the same weights in your browser, so nothing you type leaves your device.
This is a learning project built on a public research dataset of adult women of Pima Indian heritage. It is not a medical device and cannot diagnose diabetes. Please see a clinician for any health decision.
